Frequently asked questions

How do I pay?

Choose a product, enter your phone number and email, then pay on the Paystack checkout. It shows the payment options available to you.

What happens after I pay?

We confirm your payment with Paystack, then you will see a confirmation with your reference. Tap "Send order on WhatsApp" so we get your details and deliver.

I paid but did not see the confirmation. What should I do?

Do not pay again. Tap "Verify" at the top, send us your payment reference on WhatsApp and we will check it.

Can I track my order?

Yes. Tap "Track" at the top and message us your reference on WhatsApp.

Is my information safe?

Payments are handled by Paystack and prices are set on our server, so they cannot be changed from your browser. Your Ghana Card number, if needed, goes only into the WhatsApp message to us.
